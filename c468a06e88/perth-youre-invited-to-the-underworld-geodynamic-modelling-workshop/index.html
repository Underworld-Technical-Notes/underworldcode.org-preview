Underworld Geodynamic Modelling Workshop. Perth, 7 May 2025

Australian National University
April 29, 2025
https://doi.org/10.59350/8vp5e-6cx59
Photo by Marvin Meyer / Unsplash

📍 Location: Curtin University - 402.323:FF, 9am, May 7, 2025

You are invited to a full-day workshop on Underworld — a computational Geodynamics / Tectonics modelling framework. This is an excellent opportunity for researchers, students, and professionals interested in computational geodynamics and Earth science modelling to learn about Underworld.

This hands-on workshop is perfect for new and prospective users, with training provided to help you get started with Underworld. You’ll learn how to use the software, explore its latest features, and see how it’s being applied to cutting-edge geoscience problems.

It’s also a great chance for the Underworld development team to showcase recent progress, share insights, and connect with the Curtin research community.

Register now !

All are welcome – no prior experience required!
